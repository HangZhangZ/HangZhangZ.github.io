01 / Approach

GenAI:
Interactive, Informative, Suggestive.

Interactive systems should accept incomplete and multimodal guidance and allow designers to decide what to resolve next.

Informative systems should connect spatial exploration to fabrication, material, performance, or production knowledge.

Suggestive systems should offer relevant alternatives without collapsing design into automated optimization.

Together, these roles position computation as a collaborative design infrastructure for framing, comparing, and revising decisions rather than replacing design judgment.
